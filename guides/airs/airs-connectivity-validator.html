Connectivity Validator

Generate the commands that prove a pillar is reachable from your environment, then paste the output back to find out what broke.

How this works, and what it never asks for

Pick a pillar, fill in the handful of values that identify your tenant, and the page writes a ladder of commands. Run them from the machine that will actually carry the traffic, paste what comes back, and the decoder names the layer that failed and what to do about it.

It never asks for a credential. Secrets are referenced as shell variables you set yourself, so no key, token or secret is ever typed into this page. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you enter or paste is sent anywhere, and nothing is stored.

Run these where the traffic will originate

Egress policy, proxies and TLS inspection are properties of a network location, not of an account. A command that succeeds on a laptop and fails in a build runner has told you nothing except that the two places are different. Run it from the pod, the runner, or the host that will carry the real traffic.

Paste everything a command printed, including the lines beginning with *, > and <. Those carry the name resolution, the TLS handshake and the status line, which is what makes the difference between a useful verdict and a guess. One command at a time gives the clearest answer.
